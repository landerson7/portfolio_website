# Asset credits

- The interactive map layouts and lighting are drawn in `src/art.js`. The world props and terrain are original generated pixel art, sliced into the compressed WebP sheets `realm-atlas.webp` and `realm-terrain.webp`. The map combines separate textures and sprites with the original movement geometry.
- The art prompts covered an emerald and violet forest, cobble paths and water, a book-filled cottage and park paintings, academy residents, a flat overhead aircraft and four workbenches, medieval market buildings, and gold and sapphire interface details. The knight and longhorn are independent stylizations. The game is unaffiliated with the universities.
- The Home, Academy, Lab and Village thumbnails were generated for this project using a single four-panel prompt describing an overhead book-filled cottage, a library academy, a flat aircraft hangar and a medieval market. They were cropped and reduced to 240 × 112 pixels with a limited palette. They are navigation illustrations.
- The landscape thumbnail, terrain textures and UI emblems reuse original generated art from the existing portfolio. Local copies keep the game self-contained.
- Pixelify Sans and Source Sans 3 are distributed under the SIL Open Font License. License text is included with each font.
- The current software résumé is the supplied `UTAustinResume2.pdf`. The older résumé remains available at its historical file paths.
